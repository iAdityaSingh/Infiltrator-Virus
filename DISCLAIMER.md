# Disclaimer and Safe-Use Boundaries

Benign simulation for cybersecurity education and detection engineering.

- Generates synthetic events only. No keyboard hook, no screen or clipboard capture.
- No persistence, hiding, auto-execution, or embedding in PDF/image files.
- Network traffic is hard-coded to `127.0.0.1`; there is no setting for an external host.
- Do not adapt it to capture real input or transmit real data.
- Test detections only on systems you own or are explicitly authorised to assess.
